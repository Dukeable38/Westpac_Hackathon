Every invoice Radar checks before you pay gets a fingerprint: a code unique to that exact file. Radar keeps the fingerprint, not the invoice or its file name.

If someone later says an invoice was different, or that you still owe more, drop their copy on the payment. Radar tells you whether it's the exact invoice you checked and paid.
